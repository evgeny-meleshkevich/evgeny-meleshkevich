### 👨‍💻 About Me

I design and develop **autonomous AI agents**, **data processing pipelines**, and **automation tools** that solve practical business problems. My work focuses on connecting business requirements with verifiable engineering — combining LLMs, deterministic business logic, and cloud infrastructure.

- 🌐 **Interactive Portfolio:** Explore production case studies at **[evgeny-meleshkevich.github.io](https://evgeny-meleshkevich.github.io/)**
- 🤖 **Core Focus:** Autonomous AI Agents, LLM Orchestration, Computer Vision & Super-Resolution
- ⚙️ **Engineering:** High-throughput Data Extraction, Background Workers, Cloud GPU Inference (Modal, Kaggle)
- 🎯 **Engineering Principles:** Measurable outcomes, automated verification, reproducible workflows
